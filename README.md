<h2>What Are SARMs?</h2>
<p>
  SARMs, or Selective Androgen Receptor Modulators, are a class of compounds that mimic testosterone’s effects. They work by binding to specific androgen receptors in muscle and bone tissue, which results in increased protein synthesis, faster muscle recovery, and improved strength. Unlike anabolic steroids, SARMs target muscle tissues more selectively, which lowers the risk of many unwanted side effects. Throughout my cycles, I have found that SARMs can be a powerful tool when used responsibly.
</p>
